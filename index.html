Terms & Privacy

What WorkMatch is

WorkMatch matches your answers against publicly listed open remote jobs and shows you the ones most likely to fit, with a plain-language reason why. Listings are sourced live via Jobicy's public jobs API; applying always takes you to the original posting to complete with the employer directly — we don't run our own application flow.

Payment & refunds

The $2 unlock is a one-time charge, processed through PayPal Checkout — you can pay with a PayPal account or, where PayPal makes it available for your location and card, as a guest with a debit/credit card. WorkMatch's server never sees or stores your card details; that's handled entirely by PayPal. Your unlock is verified and recorded by our server once PayPal confirms the payment, then saved to this device so it won't re-lock on return visits. Because this delivers instantly and in full, all sales are final; we don't offer refunds once the list is unlocked. If a charge failed or something's genuinely wrong on our end, contact us and we'll sort it out.

Your data

Your answers (name, work type, experience, skills, availability) are used only to fetch and rank job matches for you. They're stored in your browser on this device, not on a WorkMatch server or database, and aren't sold or shared with third parties. Payment details are handled entirely by PayPal under their own privacy policy.

Job listings

Listings, pay ranges, and job details come from third-party employers via Jobicy and may change or close without notice. WorkMatch doesn't employ, vet, or guarantee any listed role — always use your own judgment when applying and sharing information with an employer.
